NCT ID: NCT05247437
Brief Title: Digital Lifestyle Intervention For Diabetes/Pre-Diabetes
Official Title: A Pilot Cluster Randomized Controlled Trial of a Digital Lifestyle Intervention for Individuals With Prediabetes or Type 2 Diabetes
Status: UNKNOWN
Phase: NA
Conditions: Type 2 Diabetes, PreDiabetes, Obesity
Sponsor: The University of Hong Kong

Brief Summary:
The study aims to evaluate the acceptability, feasibility, and preliminary efficacy of a digital lifestyle intervention, called Fitness Digital (FitD), for individuals with prediabetes or type 2 diabetes.

Eligibility Criteria:
Inclusion Criteria:

* Age 18+
* Hong Kong resident
* Can understand Chinese and Cantonese
* Have self-reported diagnosis of type 2 diabetes or prediabetes
* Have a smart phone and able to use a mobile app

Exclusion Criteria:

* None
